## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security of our software seriously. If you believe you have found a security vulnerability, please follow the steps below to report it responsibly.

### How to Report
Do **not** open a public GitHub issue for security-related items. Instead, please send a detailed report to **security@example.com**. 

To help us triage the issue quickly, please include:
* **A descriptive title** (e.g., "SQL Injection in User Login Field").
* **The affected version(s)** of the software.
* **Step-by-step instructions** to reproduce the vulnerability (Proof of Concept).
* **Potential impact** if the vulnerability is exploited.

### Our Process
As part of our commitment to a **Purple Team** philosophy, we value the collaboration between independent researchers and our internal development team throughout the **SDLC**.

1.  **Acknowledgment:** You will receive an automated receipt immediately, followed by a personal response from our security team within **48 hours**.
2.  **Validation:** We will investigate the report and notify you whether the vulnerability is accepted or declined.
3.  **Remediation:** If accepted, we will work on a fix to be included in the **Maintenance** phase of our lifecycle.
4.  **Disclosure:** We follow a coordinated disclosure policy. We ask that you do not disclose the vulnerability publicly until a fix has been deployed and we have reached a mutual agreement on the disclosure timeline.

### Recognition
With your permission, we credit researchers who discover significant vulnerabilities in our release notes once the patch is live.
